# EduLove - Community Guidelines

**Last Updated:** January 15, 2026

## Welcome to EduLove!

Our community thrives when members treat each other with respect and kindness. These guidelines help ensure a safe, inclusive environment for all users.

## Core Values

🤝 **Respect** - Treat others as you'd like to be treated
🛡️ **Safety** - Report suspicious or harmful behavior
🌈 **Inclusion** - Welcome all backgrounds, orientations, and identities
✨ **Authenticity** - Be genuine and honest in your interactions

---

## Prohibited Behavior

### Harassment & Abuse
❌ Bullying, name-calling, or threats
❌ Discriminatory comments (race, gender, sexuality, etc.)
❌ Stalking or repeated unwanted contact
❌ Sexual harassment or coercion

### Fraud & Deception
❌ Fake profiles or catfishing
❌ Impersonation of others
❌ Misrepresenting your age (must be 18+)
❌ Fraudulent verification attempts

### Explicit Content
❌ Sexually explicit photos or videos
❌ Solicitation of sexual content
❌ Nude images without consent
❌ Links to adult content

### Spam & Scams
❌ Promotional spam or advertising
❌ Multi-level marketing (MLM) schemes
❌ Requests for money or personal information
❌ Phishing links or malware

### Violence & Dangerous Content
❌ Threats of violence
❌ Glorification of violence
❌ Self-harm content
❌ Illegal activities

---

## Community Standards by Feature

### Profile Information
✅ Use a real or consistent nickname
✅ Upload appropriate, recent photos
✅ Be honest about university and course
✅ Write genuine bios and interests

### Messaging
✅ Keep conversations respectful
✅ Honor "no thanks" responses
✅ Block or report unwanted users
✅ Never share personal location

### Matching System
✅ Use for genuine connections
✅ Respect when others aren't interested
✅ Don't mass-like for matches
✅ Report suspicious matching patterns

## Consequences for Violations

### First Offense
⚠️ Warning notice
⚠️ Account restrictions
⚠️ Required community guidelines review

### Second Offense
🚫 Temporary suspension (24-72 hours)
🚫 Loss of messaging privileges
🚫 Photo/profile restrictions

### Serious/Repeated Violations
🚫 **Permanent account ban**
🚫 IP address ban
🚫 Report to law enforcement (if necessary)

## How to Report

**Found a violation? Report it immediately.**

1. **In-App Reporting**
   - Open user's profile
   - Tap "Report User"
   - Select reason and provide details

2. **Direct Report**
   - Email: report@edulove.com
   - Include username and details

3. **Emergency**
   - For threats of violence: Contact law enforcement
   - Include EduLove account info in report

---

## Special Protections

### 18+ Verification
- All users must be 18+
- Age verification is mandatory
- Underage users will be immediately banned

### Student-Only Community
- University verification required
- Protects against fake profiles
- Creates safe student-focused environment

### Consent & Boundaries
- No photos without permission
- Respect "not interested" responses
- No unsolicited explicit messages
- Block/mute available for all users

### Hookups & Casual Dating
- Hookups and casual dating are allowed when clearly communicated and consensual between adults (18+).
- Always be explicit about intentions and respect boundaries; 'no' means 'no'.
- Meet in public places first, tell a friend where you're going, and avoid sharing private or financial information.
- Report any coercion, pressure, or solicitation—this is strictly prohibited and will be enforced with account sanctions.

---

## Appeals Process

Believe an action was taken in error? Appeal within 14 days:

1. Email: appeals@edulove.com
2. Include your username and case details
3. Explain why action should be reversed
4. Review completed within 5 business days
